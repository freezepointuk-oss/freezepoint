Don’t Keep Regassing If There’s a Hidden Fault

If your AC stops cooling soon after a refill, the gas may not be the real problem. Your vehicle could have a leak, pressure issue, compressor fault or another AC system problem.

FreezePoint helps you choose the right service first, so you avoid wasting money on repeat regas appointments when your system may need proper testing.

❄️ AC blowing warm or only slightly cool air
⏱️ Cooling stops again soon after regas
🔍 Gas keeps running out quickly
⚙️ AC makes noise or does not switch on
💨 Airflow is fine but the air is not cold

What Could Your Car AC Need?

Every AC problem is different. That is why FreezePoint offers more than just regas. We help identify the most sensible next step based on your symptoms.

AC Regas For vehicles that need a gas refill to restore cooling performance. Leak Diagnosis For systems losing gas or failing to stay cold after refill. Pressure Testing For checking whether the AC system can hold pressure properly. Hydrogen Leak Testing For accurate leak detection, including smaller or harder-to-find leaks. Compressor Diagnosis For AC not switching on, weak cooling or suspected compressor faults. Mobile AC Service For convenient service at home, work or roadside where possible.
