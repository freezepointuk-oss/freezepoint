Before Regassing Your Vehicle

A regas is suitable when the AC system is simply low on refrigerant. However, if your vehicle AC loses gas quickly, stops cooling soon after a refill, or does not hold pressure, a leak diagnosis or pressure test may be required first. This helps avoid wasting gas and helps identify the real fault properly.
